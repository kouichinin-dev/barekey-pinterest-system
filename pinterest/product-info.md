# barekey — Product Info (Pinterest)

## Product
Ultra-thin 0.12mm individual MacBook keyboard skins. Matte finish. Applied key by key.

## Key specs
- Thickness: 0.12mm (base 0.09mm + adhesive)
- Finish: matte, anti-oil, anti-fingerprint
- Backlight: 15% light transmission — keys visible in the dark
- Adhesive: zero residue, peels off clean after months of use
- Fit: precision laser-cut, individual keys, edges sit flush
- Feel: keystroke rebound identical to stock, airflow unobstructed

## Why it's better than alternatives
- Full silicone cover: ruins typing feel, traps heat, leaves screen marks — ours doesn't
- Printed sticker skins: thicker, peel over time, fade — ours is single-layer PVC
- Bare keys: ABS plastic absorbs oil and gets shiny permanently — ours protects from day one

## Target customer
MacBook users who care about aesthetics and typing feel. Perfectionists. People who've tried other solutions and been disappointed.
